THE ARENA

Where Wall Street ruthlessness meets computational engineering precision.

Welcome to WALL STREET ARENA — the flagship technical finance showdown organized by PCET's Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE) Department of Computer Engineering in association with ACM-W PCCOE and CESA.

Forget textbook exercises. This is a battleground engineered for real-time strategic problem solvers, quantitative analysts, algorithmic thinkers, and market strategists. Across two ruthless competitive rounds, syndicates will decrypt volatile macroeconomic scenarios, analyze high-speed tickers, construct winning portfolios, and execute tactical decisions under the clock.

"You don't need a stockbroker's license to conquer the floor — you need numerical agility, nerves of steel, and razor-sharp synergy."

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Students from all engineering, technology, and management branches across recognized universities and colleges are welcome. Teams of 2 or 3 members may comprise students from different academic years or departments.
Round 1 focuses on quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, numerical patterns, probability, basic macroeconomic principles, and analytical deduction. It is designed to evaluate raw problem-solving speed and strategic logic.
Round 2 is conducted at PCCOE Lab 6221L - 6222L on pre-configured high-speed workstations with dedicated LAN connectivity. Teams may also carry personal laptops as backup if permitted by coordinating faculty.
Winning syndicates are evaluated by their cumulative portfolio valuation (P&L), risk-adjusted Sharpe performance, adherence to risk limits, and strategic execution during unexpected breaking news market shocks.
